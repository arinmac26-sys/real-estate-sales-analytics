# KPI Definitions

- **Total Sales** = `SUM(Sale Amount)`
- **Transactions** = `COUNT(DISTINCT Serial Number)`
- **Average Sale** = `Total Sales / Transactions`
- **Average Sales Ratio** = `AVG(Sales Ratio)`
- **Sales Contribution %** = `Segment Sales / Total Sales × 100`
- **MoM Growth %** = `(Current Month Sales / Prior Month Sales - 1) × 100`
- **Data Quality Rate** = `Valid Records / Total Records × 100`
